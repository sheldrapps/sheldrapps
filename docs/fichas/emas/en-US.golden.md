# EPUB Merge & Split — en-US Golden Play Store Listing

## Purpose

Create a Play Store listing that clearly positions EPUB Merge & Split as a focused utility for reorganizing EPUB content and creating new EPUB files.

The app has two main jobs:

**Merge multiple EPUB files into one organized book.**

**Split one EPUB into smaller books using chapters, sections, equal parts, custom points, or a maximum file size.**

The listing should make the app feel flexible, controlled, private, and easy to use. It should not present EPUB Merge & Split as a full ebook editor, ebook reader, file converter, DRM tool, cloud service, or AI content generator.

The core message should feel like this:

**Combine several EPUBs into one, or divide a large EPUB exactly the way you need. Organize the content, choose the result cover, and save new EPUB files directly on your device.**

---

## App Name

EPUB Merge & Split

## Why

“EPUB Merge & Split” clearly communicates both primary functions and includes the strongest search term: **EPUB**.

It is direct, understandable, and more descriptive than using the internal acronym “EMAS” as the public-facing name.

The name supports searches related to:

* merging EPUB files
* combining ebooks
* splitting EPUB files
* dividing books by chapter
* creating smaller EPUB files

No alternate app name is needed for en-US.

## Why

## Long Description

Merge multiple EPUB files into one book or split a large EPUB into smaller, organized files.

EPUB Merge & Split gives you flexible control over how your EPUB content is combined and divided.

Choose several EPUB files from your device, place them in the order you want, select the result cover, and create one new EPUB containing all of them.

You can also choose a single EPUB and divide it using the method that best fits your book or device.

With EPUB Merge & Split, you can:

* Merge multiple EPUB files into one book
* Change the order of EPUBs before merging
* Choose and adjust the cover of the merged EPUB
* Split an EPUB by chapters
* Split an EPUB by detected sections
* Divide an EPUB into equal parts
* Add custom split points
* Split an EPUB using a maximum file size
* Review how the resulting parts will be organized
* Save newly created EPUB files
* Open completed EPUBs with compatible apps
* Share created EPUB files
* Find saved results in My EPUBs
* Change language and theme settings
* Remove ads with an upgrade option

Use chapter or section splitting when you want each structural part to become its own EPUB.

Use equal parts when you want a specific number of similarly sized books.

Use custom split points when you want full control over where each new EPUB begins and ends.

Use maximum-size splitting when you need each resulting file to stay below a selected size.

EPUB Merge & Split creates new result files from the EPUBs you select. Your source books remain available separately.

File processing happens on your device.

No accounts.
No book uploads.
Private control over your EPUB files.

The app is useful for readers, students, writers, researchers, collectors, and anyone who manages large books, collections, compilations, or serialized content.

Whether you need to combine a group of EPUBs into one organized volume or divide a large book into easier-to-manage parts, EPUB Merge & Split provides a direct workflow without complicated desktop tools.

The app focuses on one practical goal:

**organize EPUB content and create the exact EPUB files you need.**
