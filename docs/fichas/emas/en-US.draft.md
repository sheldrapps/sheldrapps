## Long Description

Text:
Merge multiple EPUB files into one organized book, or split a large EPUB into new files that fit your needs.

EPUB Merge & Split gives you control over the order, division method, cover, and final files. Choose several EPUBs, arrange them, and create one new book. Or choose one EPUB and divide it by chapters, sections, equal parts, custom split points, or a maximum file size.

With EPUB Merge & Split you can:

- Merge several EPUB files into one book
- Reorder EPUBs before merging
- Choose and adjust the result cover
- Split by chapters or detected sections
- Split into equal parts
- Add custom split points
- Use a maximum file size as a practical limit
- Review how the resulting parts are organized
- Save, open, and share newly created EPUB files
- Find saved results in My EPUBs

Your source books remain available separately. File processing for merging and splitting happens on your device. No account is required, and EPUB content is not uploaded for these operations.

Use it to organize collections, combine related books, divide large books, or create more manageable EPUB parts without a complicated desktop workflow.
